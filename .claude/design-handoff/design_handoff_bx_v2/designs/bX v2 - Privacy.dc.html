Short version

We collect what you send us, your contact details, your message, your CV, and nothing else beyond basic, aggregated site analytics. We do not sell data, we do not run advertising trackers, and you can ask us to delete your record at any time by writing to info@bxtechnologies.net.

Who we are

bX Technologies Pvt Ltd is a software development company incorporated in Pakistan in March 2019 and registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. We operate a delivery office in Islamabad, Pakistan and a client-facing presence in Duisburg, Germany. For the purposes of data protection law, bX Technologies Pvt Ltd is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Where we process personal data on behalf of a client as part of a delivery engagement, that client is the controller and we act as a processor under the terms of our contract with them. This policy covers only the data we handle in our own right.

What we collect

We collect three categories of information, and in each case only what is needed for the purpose.

  • Enquiries. When you use the contact form or email us: your name, company, work email, telephone number if you give one, the nature of your enquiry, the company-size and budget indications you select, and the message you write.
  • Job applications. When you apply through the careers pages: your name, contact details, location, links you provide, years of experience, your CV, and your written answers. We do not ask for date of birth, marital status, photographs or national identity numbers at application stage.
  • Site usage. Aggregated, non-identifying statistics about pages viewed, referring source and approximate region, plus the technical logs our hosting provider keeps for security and fault diagnosis. Details of the specific cookies involved are in our cookies policy.

If you subscribe to our insights digest we hold your email address and the date you confirmed the subscription, and nothing else.

Why we hold it

We rely on the following grounds. Each purpose is limited to the data listed above.

  • To answer you. Replying to an enquiry, arranging a call, and preparing a proposal, on the basis of your request and our legitimate interest in responding to it.
  • To assess an application. Reviewing your CV, running the interview process and making a hiring decision, on the basis of taking steps prior to a possible employment contract.
  • To operate and improve the site. Understanding which pages are read and keeping the site secure, on the basis of our legitimate interest, using aggregated data.
  • To send what you asked for. The insights digest, on the basis of the consent you gave when subscribing, withdrawable from any email or by writing to us.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not use it to train machine-learning models.

How long we keep it

Enquiries
Twenty-four months from the last contact, then deleted, or for the life of the engagement plus our contractual retention period if you become a client.
Job applications
Twelve months from the decision, so the team can contact you about later roles. Deleted sooner on request.
Digest subscription
Until you unsubscribe, plus a suppression record so we do not email you again by mistake.
Server and security logs
Ninety days.

Who else sees it

Your information is visible to the bX people who need it for the purpose it was collected for, for an enquiry, the directors and the engineering lead who would run the work; for an application, the hiring team. Beyond that we use a small number of service providers under contract: our website host, our email provider, our applicant tracking and our analytics provider. Each is bound to process data only on our instructions.

Where an engagement is delivered with our partner Vis Group in Germany, contract and contact details may be shared with them for that engagement, and only for that engagement. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

Where it is processed

We are a Pakistan-registered company with a German presence, and our teams in both countries may access enquiry and application data. Where personal data originating in the European Economic Area is transferred to Pakistan, we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and apply the access controls described below.

For client project data handled under a delivery contract, we can commit to EU-region processing and to a stated list of which providers and models ever see that data. Those commitments sit in the engagement contract, not in this policy.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable law, you can ask us to: confirm what we hold about you and give you a copy; correct anything inaccurate; delete your record; restrict how we use it; or object to our processing it on legitimate-interest grounds. Where you gave consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Write to info@bxtechnologies.net and we will respond within thirty days. We do not charge for this and we do not require a particular form of words. If you are in the European Economic Area and you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your national supervisory authority.

Security

Access to enquiry and application data is limited to named people and reviewed when someone changes role or leaves. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our providers, administrative accounts require multi-factor authentication, and CVs and attachments are held in the applicant system rather than circulated by email. If a breach affects your data and presents a risk to you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Changes

We update this policy when what we do changes. The date at the top of the page is the date of the current version, and material changes will be noted here for at least ninety days. Continuing to use the site after a change means the updated policy applies to you.
