<H2 id="what">What a cookie is</H2>

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store, so that it can recognise the same browser on a later page or visit. Some are necessary for the site to function; others are optional and only run with your permission. The same rules apply to similar technologies such as local storage, which this site does not use.

<H2 id="essential">Essential cookie</H2>

This one is set because the banner needs to remember what you chose. It holds only your choice, carries no personal data, and requires no consent.

<H2 id="analytics">Analytics</H2>

We count page views with Plausible Analytics, a service run from the European Union by Plausible Insights OÜ in Estonia. It does not set cookies or use local storage, and it does not build a profile of you. It counts page views, referring sites, browser and device type and approximate country, in aggregate. Your IP address is used only to tell one day's visitors apart and is not stored.

Even so, we load it only after you accept in the banner. Until then, and if you decline, no analytics code runs on this site.

<H2 id="forms">Spam protection on forms</H2>

Our contact, application and digest forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a submission comes from a person. It runs only on pages with a form, processes your IP address and browser characteristics for that check, and sets no cookies on this site. It keeps the forms usable, so it is not something the banner switches off.

<H2 id="none">What we do not use</H2>

- **No advertising cookies.** We do not run ads and we do not place retargeting pixels.
- **No social embeds that track.** Links to LinkedIn, Facebook and X are plain links, not embedded widgets, so those networks do not see your visit.
- **No cross-site identifiers or data-broker tags,** and no session recording or heat-mapping.
- **No fingerprinting** as a substitute for the cookies you declined.

<H2 id="control">Changing your choice</H2>

The banner on your first visit lets you accept or decline analytics. Declining leaves only the essential cookie in place, and nothing on the site stops working. You can change your mind at any time from Cookie settings in the footer of every page, or delete the `bx_consent` cookie and the banner will ask again.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a decline and do not show the banner.

<H2 id="browser">Browser controls</H2>

Every major browser lets you see the cookies a site has set, delete them, and refuse new ones, usually under Settings, then Privacy. You can also browse in a private window, which discards cookies when you close it. Blocking all cookies affects most websites, not only this one.

<H2 id="changes">Changes</H2>

If we add or remove a cookie, this page is updated before the change goes live and the date at the top changes with it. Where a new cookie needs consent, the banner will ask again rather than relying on your earlier choice. What we do with the data collected is covered by our [privacy policy](/privacy).
