<H2 id="what">What we collect</H2>

We collect three categories of information, and in each case only what is needed for the purpose.

- **Enquiries.** When you use a contact form or email us: your name, company, work email, telephone number if you give one, the nature of your enquiry, the options you select (such as company size, budget, project stage or timing, and how you heard of us), and the message you write.
- **Job applications.** When you apply through the careers pages: your name, contact details, location, links you provide, years of experience, when you could start, how you heard of us, your CV, and your written answers. We do not ask for date of birth, marital status, photographs or national identity numbers at application stage.
- **Site usage.** If you accept analytics: aggregated, non-identifying statistics about pages viewed, referring source and approximate country, from Plausible, which sets no cookies. On pages with a form: the IP address and browser characteristics that Cloudflare Turnstile checks to keep out automated spam. And the technical logs our hosting provider keeps for security and fault diagnosis. Our fonts are served from our own server, so your browser makes no requests to Google or other font services. Details are in our [cookies policy](/cookies).

If you sign up for our insights digest, we receive your email address and the date you signed up, and nothing else. We have not started sending the digest yet. Before the first issue we will email you to confirm that you want it, and every issue will include a link to unsubscribe.

<H2 id="why">Why we hold it</H2>

We rely on the following legal grounds under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which applies to visitors in the European Economic Area. Each purpose is limited to the data listed above.

- **To answer you.** Replying to an enquiry, arranging a call, and preparing a proposal: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and otherwise our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **To assess an application.** Reviewing your CV, running the interview process and making a hiring decision: steps taken at your request before a possible employment contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **To keep the site and forms secure.** Spam protection on forms and security logs: our legitimate interest in a site that works and is not abused (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **To improve the site.** Understanding which pages are read: the consent you give in the cookie banner (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), withdrawable at any time from Cookie settings in the footer.
- **To send what you asked for.** The insights digest: the consent you give when you sign up and confirm (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), withdrawable from any issue or by writing to us.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not use it to train machine-learning models.

<H2 id="share">Who else sees it</H2>

Your information is visible to the BX people who need it for the purpose it was collected for, for an enquiry, the directors and the engineering lead who would run the work; for an application, our HR lead and the engineering lead. Beyond that we use a small number of service providers under contract, each bound to process data only on our instructions:

- **Our website host,** which serves the site and keeps its technical logs.
- **Resend,** which delivers what you send through our forms, including CVs as attachments, to our inbox.
- **Cloudflare,** whose Turnstile service checks form submissions for spam.
- **Plausible,** our analytics provider, only if you accept analytics.

Where an engagement is delivered with our partner Vis Group in Germany, contract and contact details may be shared with them for that engagement, and only for that engagement. **We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers.**

<H2 id="transfer">Where it is processed</H2>

We are a company registered in Pakistan, so what you send us is accessed by our team in Pakistan. Pakistan does not have an adequacy decision from the European Commission; the protections described under Security apply to your data there, and where we receive personal data from a client or partner in the European Economic Area we agree the European Commission's standard contractual clauses with them. Resend and Cloudflare are based in the United States; where they process personal data from the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses. Plausible processes analytics data within the European Union.

For client project data handled under a delivery contract, we can commit to EU-region processing and to a stated list of which providers and models ever see that data. Those commitments sit in the engagement contract, not in this policy.

<H2 id="rights">Your rights</H2>

Subject to the applicable law, you can ask us to: confirm what we hold about you and give you a copy; correct anything inaccurate; delete your record; restrict how we use it; give you the data you provided in a common machine-readable format, or send it to another organisation (data portability); or object at any time to our processing it on legitimate-interest grounds. Where you gave consent, you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting what we did before you withdrew it.

Write to [info@bxtechnologies.net](mailto:info@bxtechnologies.net) and we will respond within thirty days. We do not charge for this and we do not require a particular form of words.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work or where you believe your rights were infringed. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first, but you do not have to come to us before you complain.

<H2 id="security">Security</H2>

Access to enquiry and application data is limited to named people and reviewed when someone changes role or leaves. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our providers, administrative accounts require multi-factor authentication, and CVs arrive in a restricted mailbox that only our HR lead and the engineering lead can open, from which they are deleted at the end of the retention period. If a breach affects your data and presents a risk to you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

<H2 id="changes">Changes</H2>

We update this policy when what we do changes. The date at the top of the page is the date of the current version, and material changes will be noted here for at least ninety days. Where a change affects something you consented to, we will ask for your consent again rather than assume it.
